Ranking Methodology

This ranking counts publications related to Knowledge Graphs, Semantic Web, ontologies, knowledge representation, entity linking, knowledge graph reasoning, knowledge graph embeddings, KG question answering, and related topics.

  • Only publications within the selected year range are considered.
  • Publications are identified using KG-related keywords and core KG venues.
  • Every retained publication contributes exactly one point.
  • Researcher score equals the number of retained KG-related publications.
  • Researchers are sorted in descending order of their scores.
  • Tied researchers receive the same rank.
